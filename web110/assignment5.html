Lastly, I learned social media is more than just a way to communicate or share content. The Honeycomb model explains social media through seven important elements: identity, conversations, sharing, presence, relationships, reputation, and groups. I learned that these elements describe different ways people interact and participate on social media. Identity focuses on how users present themselves, while conversations involve communicating with others. Sharing refers to exchanging information and content, and presence shows how users make themselves available online. Relationships involve creating and maintaining connections, while reputation relates to how users and their activities are perceived by others. Groups involve forming communities around common interests or topics. I also learned that social media platforms do not necessarily give equal importance to all seven elements. Some platforms support many of them, while others are more focused on specific functions. Overall, the Honeycomb Model helped me understand the different purposes and characteristics of social media platforms.
