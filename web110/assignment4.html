Viewing email messages headers is very important topic on this lesson. I learned that email spoofing occurs when someone creates a message that appears to come from another sender. Email spoofing is dangerous not just because it is a scam but because it can steal your information once you get deceived because scammers can make emails look as though they were sent by a trusted person. However, checking only the “From” address is not always enough to determine whether a message is legitimate. Email headers provide additional details that can help reveal where a message originated. For example, on Gmail, I can open an email, select the three-dot menu, and choose “Show original” to view the complete header. This information may include the sending server, IP address, or authentication results. Reviewing these details can be helpful when investigating a suspicious message or email because spoofed emails are often used in phishing and other scams, understanding how to examine email headers can help me identify potentially unsafe messages.
