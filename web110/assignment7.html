Learning the Network Interface Controller (NIC) taught me that it is an important computer component that allows devices to connect to a network and communicate with other devices through wired or wireless connections. I realized that without a working network interface, a computer would have difficulty accessing the internet and sharing information with other devices. I also learned that most modern computers already have built-in network interfaces, such as Ethernet and Wi-Fi adapters, which help send and receive data. This topic helped me understand that even though a NIC is not something we usually notice when using a computer, it plays an important role in keeping us connected. As someone who wants to become a web developer, I envision myself using this knowledge to better understand how computers connect to servers and access websites. I can apply what I learned in my current studies by understanding basic network connections and troubleshooting internet problems, while in the future, I can use this knowledge to develop websites, identify connectivity issues, and ensure that my projects work properly online. From studying Virtual Private Networks (VPNs), I learned that they help protect our online privacy by creating an encrypted connection between our devices and a VPN server. This makes it more difficult for unauthorized people to access or read our data while it travels across the internet. I also learned that VPNs are useful when connecting to public Wi-Fi and when employees need to access their company’s private network while working remotely. However, I realized that using a VPN does not guarantee complete online safety, so we still need to use strong passwords, visit secure websites, and be careful when sharing personal information. As someone who wants to become a web developer, I see this knowledge as important because developers must understand how to protect information and maintain users’ trust. I can apply what I learned in my current studies by practicing safe internet habits and understanding online security. In the future, I can use this knowledge to help develop secure websites, protect sensitive data, and support safer online communication.
